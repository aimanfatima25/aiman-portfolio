01 · About Me

Building automations that think and connect

I'm Aiman Fatima, an AI Automation & Workflow Builder. I focus on combining workflow tools like n8n with AI models like Claude and custom APIs to create systems that save time and reduce manual effort.

I enjoy breaking a process down into clear steps, figuring out where AI can add real value, and connecting everything so it runs smoothly in the background. I also build lightweight backend services with FastAPI when a workflow needs custom logic.

My approach is simple: understand the problem first, keep the design clean, and build something that is reliable and easy to maintain.
